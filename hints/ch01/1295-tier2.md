# 1295. Find Numbers with Even Number of Digits — Tier 2 (observation)

The three families:

1. **Convert to text** and measure its length.
2. **Divide it down** — strip one digit at a time until nothing is left, counting as you go.
3. **Use the magnitude directly** — digit count is a function of the number's size, and there's a
   standard-library function that gets at size.

Write whichever came to you first. Get it green. Then implement the other two — comparing them is
what this problem is actually for.

> **The official hints, verbatim:** "How to compute the number of digits of a number?" and
> "Divide the number by 10 again and again to get the number of digits."
>
> That's family 2 only. It's the intended solution, and it is not the best one available given
> these constraints — see Tier 4.
