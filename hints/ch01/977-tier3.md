# 977. Squares of a Sorted Array — Tier 3 (approach)

Two pointers, `left` at index 0 and `right` at the last index. Compare the two candidates, take
the larger, advance that pointer inward. Repeat until they cross.

That produces the answer in **descending** order, which is backwards from what you need to return.
Two ways out of that. Think about what each one costs before you pick.

> This problem ships with **no official hints** — it's the one you're expected to work out
> unaided. Its official topic tags are *Array*, *Two Pointers*, and *Sorting*, which between them
> give away roughly what Tiers 2 and 3 do.
