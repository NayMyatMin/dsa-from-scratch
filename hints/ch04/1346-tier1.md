# 1346. Check If N and Its Double Exist — Tier 1 (nudge)

Answer these three by hand and write each one down before you look at the next: `[10, 2, 5, 3]`,
`[0, 3]`, `[3, 0, 0]`.

The last two disagree, and both of them hold a zero. Name the word in the statement that separates
them. It is not a word about values.

Now take 10 and 5 out of the first list. One of those two is the one being doubled. Say which — then
say whether you could have known that while holding only the 10.
