# 1299. Replace Elements with Greatest Element on Right Side — Tier 1 (nudge)

Take `[17, 18, 5, 4, 6, 1]` and say what index 4 becomes, then index 3, then index 2 — those three,
out loud, and for each one name the indices whose values decided it.

Now `[5, 4, 3]`. Say what index 0 becomes, and say which word in the statement stops the answer from
being 5.

Then `[7, 7, 7]`. Two of those sevens are to the right of the first one and one of them is to the
right of the second. Say what each of the three positions becomes, and say where the sevens ran out.
