# 905. Sort Array By Parity — Tier 1 (nudge)

Write out every accepted answer for `[1, 2]`, then every accepted answer for `[1, 3]`. Same length,
same kind of values, and one of them has more accepted answers than the other. Say which, and name
the thing in the input that decides it.

Now `[3, 1, 2, 4]`. The statement lists four accepted answers; find all four yourself before you
take its word for it. Then say how many `[2, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1]` has, and be careful about what you are
counting — orderings and answers are not the same thing here.

Last, and this is the one to sit with. Put `[2, 4, 1, 3]` beside `[2, 1, 4, 3]`. One is accepted and
one is not. Name the two positions that decide it — indices and values, not "it looks wrong" — and
then say what you would have to look at to be sure about a list of five thousand elements.
