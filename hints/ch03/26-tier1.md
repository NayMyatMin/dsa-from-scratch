# 26. Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array — Tier 1 (nudge)

Take `[0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4]` and do it by hand the way the word *remove* suggests: lift a
duplicate out and close the gap behind it, five times over. Count the values you slid left. Not the
removals — the slides.

Then answer the other half, which has nothing to do with cost. Go back to the untouched ten and say
what you had to read to know that an element was a duplicate. Say it as a rule, and check that the
rule would still do the same amount of reading if the list were three thousand times longer.
